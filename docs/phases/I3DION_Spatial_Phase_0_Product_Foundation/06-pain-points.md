# I3DION Spatial --- Pain Points

**Document ID:** PS-PAIN-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Hypothesis register

## 1. Purpose

This document records user problems that may justify product
capabilities. Pain points must eventually be supported by customer
evidence.

## 2. Customer Pain Points

### P1 --- Poor Spatial Understanding

Static images and text may not communicate physical scale or spatial
context effectively.

**Potential impact:** High

### P2 --- Difficult Physical Demonstration

Large equipment cannot always be transported or demonstrated.

**Potential impact:** High

### P3 --- Fragmented Product Information

Images, specifications, documents, videos, and 3D assets may live in
different places.

**Potential impact:** Medium/High

### P4 --- Repetitive Sales Explanation

Sales engineers may repeatedly explain the same physical
characteristics.

**Potential impact:** Medium

### P5 --- Limited Customer Interaction

Traditional materials provide fewer interactive ways to inspect
products.

**Potential impact:** Medium

### P6 --- Installation/Placement Uncertainty

Customers may struggle to understand how equipment relates to their
physical environment.

**Potential impact:** High for relevant products

### P7 --- Weak Engagement Measurement

Traditional brochures do not naturally provide product engagement
telemetry.

**Potential impact:** Medium

### P8 --- Slow Content Updating

Traditional catalogs can become outdated when products change.

**Potential impact:** Medium

## 3. Pain Point Scoring

Use: **Priority Score = Frequency × Severity × Business Impact × Current
Dissatisfaction**

Each factor is scored 1--5 after research.

## 4. Evidence Standard

A pain point moves from "hypothesis" to "validated" when: - Multiple
target users independently report it, - It occurs in a real workflow, -
Existing workaround is meaningful, - The user considers it important, -
There is evidence of business impact.

## 5. Anti-Pattern

Do not validate a pain point by asking: "Would you use an AR product?"

Instead ask about the current workflow and recent real examples.

## 6. Research Output

For every validated pain: - Persona - Context - Frequency - Severity -
Current workaround - Business consequence - Existing alternatives -
Evidence quote/observation - Product opportunity
