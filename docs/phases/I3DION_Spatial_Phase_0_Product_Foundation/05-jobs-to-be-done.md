**Document ID:** PS-JTBD-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Hypothesis set

## 1. JTBD Framework

We will use:

> When \[situation\], I want to \[motivation\], so I can \[expected
> outcome\].

The jobs below are initial hypotheses.

## 2. Sales Engineer --- Core Job

> When I am presenting a complex industrial product to a prospect, I
> want to demonstrate the product interactively, so the prospect can
> understand the product without relying entirely on a physical
> demonstration.

### Functional outcomes

-   Show product
-   Rotate/zoom
-   Highlight components
-   Demonstrate movement
-   Launch AR
-   Share product

### Emotional outcome

Feel confident that the customer understands the explanation.

### Social outcome

Present a modern, professional sales experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Product Marketing --- Core Job

> When I need to publish product information, I want to manage product
> assets and create digital catalogs, so sales teams can consistently
> present current information.

### Desired outcomes

-   Faster publishing
-   Fewer outdated assets
-   Centralized product information
-   Consistent branding

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Customer --- Core Job

> When I am evaluating industrial equipment, I want to explore the
> product and understand its physical and technical characteristics, so
> I can make a more confident decision.

### Desired outcomes

-   Understand product
-   Inspect visually
-   Understand scale
-   Access specifications
-   Request additional information

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Facility Manager --- Core Job

> When evaluating equipment for a facility, I want to visualize the
> equipment spatially, so I can assess whether the intended placement is
> practical.

## 6. Job Map

**Define need → Discover product → Open experience → Understand →
Explore → Visualize → Evaluate → Ask/Contact → Decide**

## 7. JTBD Validation Questions

Ask customers: 1. What triggers this job? 2. How do you perform it
today? 3. What makes it difficult? 4. What tools do you use? 5. What
outcome defines success? 6. What happens if the job goes wrong? 7. How
much time/money does the current process consume? 8. What would make you
switch tools?

## 8. Prioritization

Rank each job by: - Frequency - Importance - Dissatisfaction with
current solution - Revenue impact - Strategic fit

The highest-scoring jobs should drive MVP priorities.
