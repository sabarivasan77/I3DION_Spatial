## 3. Assumption Lifecycle

**Open → Testing → Supported / Rejected / Inconclusive**

## 4. High-Risk Rule

Critical assumptions must be tested before major irreversible
engineering investment.

## 5. Evidence Requirements

Evidence can include: - Customer interview - Usability test - Pilot -
Product analytics - Sales conversation - Competitive research -
Technical proof of concept

## 6. Decision Rule

If an assumption is rejected, related features and roadmap items must be
reconsidered.
