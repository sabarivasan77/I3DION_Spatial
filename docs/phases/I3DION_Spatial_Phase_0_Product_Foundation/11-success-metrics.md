# I3DION Spatial --- Success Metrics

**Document ID:** PS-MET-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Draft measurement framework

## 1. Measurement Philosophy

Success is not "the software is deployed."

Success means the product creates measurable user and business value.

## 2. North-Star Direction

Initial north-star hypothesis:

> **Qualified product engagement that progresses toward a meaningful
> sales action.**

The exact North Star Metric must be validated with business stakeholders
and pilot customers.

## 3. Product Health Metrics

-   Availability
-   Error rate
-   Critical defect count
-   Page/session load performance
-   3D model load success
-   AR launch success
-   Authentication success

## 4. Engagement Metrics

### Catalog

-   Catalog views
-   Unique viewers
-   Repeat viewers

### QR

-   QR scans
-   Successful QR-to-experience sessions

### Product Experience

-   Product views
-   3D viewer launches
-   Interaction rate
-   Average session duration

### AR

-   AR launch rate
-   Successful placement rate
-   Lock/unlock usage
-   Reset usage
-   AR session duration

## 5. Conversion Metrics

-   Request Quote clicks
-   Contact Sales clicks
-   Inquiry submissions
-   Qualified leads
-   Product-to-lead conversion rate

## 6. B2B Adoption Metrics

-   Active organizations
-   Active sales users
-   Active product owners
-   Products published
-   Catalogs published
-   Monthly active organizations
-   Repeat organizational usage

## 7. Quality Metrics

-   Critical production incidents
-   Mean time to detect
-   Mean time to resolve
-   Failed deployments
-   Regression defects
-   Security incidents

## 8. Suggested Funnel

**QR Scan → Product Open → 3D View → AR Launch → Engagement → CTA →
Lead**

Measure conversion at every step.

## 9. Metric Governance

Each metric requires: - Definition - Owner - Data source - Calculation -
Reporting frequency - Target/baseline - Action threshold

## 10. Baseline Rule

Do not invent targets before baseline data exists. Phase 1/early pilot
should establish realistic baselines.
