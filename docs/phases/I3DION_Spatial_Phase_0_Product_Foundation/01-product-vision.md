# I3DION Spatial --- Product Vision

**Document ID:** PS-VIS-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Phase:** Phase 0 --- Product Foundation\
**Status:** Draft for validation\
**Owner:** Product Management\
**Product:** I3DION Spatial

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the long-term direction of I3DION Spatial. It is
the strategic north star against which product decisions, roadmap items,
features, and investments should be evaluated.

## 2. Product Vision

> I3DION Spatial aims to become a scalable digital visualization
> platform for industrial products, enabling manufacturers and sales
> teams to transform traditional product information into interactive
> 3D, AR, and digital sales experiences.

The existing product baseline describes I3DION Spatial as an
**Industrial AR Catalog & Sales Visualization Platform**. The product is
intended to bridge physical industrial products and digital customer
experiences.

## 3. Vision Problem

Industrial products are often communicated through static catalogs,
images, brochures, specifications, videos, presentations, and physical
demonstrations. These formats can communicate information but do not
always provide strong spatial understanding.

The vision is therefore not simply "put a 3D model on a website." The
strategic objective is to make complex industrial products easier to
understand, evaluate, demonstrate, and act upon.

## 4. Desired Future State

A customer should be able to move from:

**Discover → Understand → Explore → Visualize → Evaluate → Contact
Sales**

without depending on a physical demonstration for every interaction.

A company should be able to move from:

**Product Data → Digital Product Experience → Customer Engagement →
Sales Inquiry**

through one governed workflow.

## 5. Strategic Direction

The product should evolve from a focused MVP into a broader industrial
visualization and sales-enablement ecosystem, while preserving the core
value proposition.

Potential future directions may include advanced configuration,
analytics, integrations, richer spatial experiences, and enterprise
collaboration. These are roadmap hypotheses, not MVP commitments.

## 6. Vision Boundaries

I3DION Spatial is not intended to become: - A generic social network - A
full CAD authoring replacement - A general-purpose ERP or CRM - A
consumer-only AR application - An engineering simulation suite

## 7. Vision Test

Every significant future feature should answer: 1. Which industrial user
benefits? 2. Which important problem does it solve? 3. How does it
strengthen visualization, evaluation, or sales enablement? 4. Can the
value be measured? 5. Does it fit the product strategy?

## 8. Validation Required

The following vision assumptions require customer discovery: -
Industrial organizations have a meaningful problem with current product
demonstration workflows. - Spatial/3D/AR experiences materially improve
product understanding. - Sales teams will adopt the workflow. -
Organizations will pay for the solution. - The problem is frequent and
valuable enough to support a sustainable product.
