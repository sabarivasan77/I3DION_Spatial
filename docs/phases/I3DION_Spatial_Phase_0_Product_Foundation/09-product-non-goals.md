# I3DION Spatial --- Product Non-Goals

**Document ID:** PS-NG-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Controlled scope document

## 1. Purpose

Non-goals prevent scope creep and protect the MVP.

## NG1 --- Not a Social Network

Community features must not transform the core product into a
social-media platform.

## NG2 --- Not a Full CAD Tool

The platform consumes and presents product assets; it is not intended to
replace professional CAD/3D authoring software.

## NG3 --- Not an ERP

Inventory, procurement, finance, manufacturing planning, and ERP
functions are outside the product boundary.

## NG4 --- Not a CRM Replacement

Lead generation may be included, but the product is not intended to
become a complete CRM.

## NG5 --- Not Engineering Simulation

The MVP is for visualization and demonstration, not engineering-grade
simulation, physics validation, or certification.

## NG6 --- Not a Generic Consumer AR Platform

The product is focused on industrial/B2B product visualization and sales
enablement.

## NG7 --- Not Unlimited Feature Collection

Every feature requires evidence, strategic fit, value, and priority.

## NG8 --- Not AI-First

AI is not automatically a product requirement. Any future AI capability
must solve a validated customer problem.

## NG9 --- Not Premature Enterprise Complexity

Enterprise architecture should be appropriate to validated needs. Avoid
building large infrastructure only because it might be useful later.

## 2. Scope Governance

A proposed feature that conflicts with a non-goal requires an explicit
product decision and documented rationale.

## 3. Change Control

Changes require: - Problem evidence - Customer impact - Business
impact - Engineering impact - Security/compliance impact - Opportunity
cost - Approval decision
