# I3DION Spatial --- Initial Product Brief

**Document ID:** PS-BRF-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Draft for Phase 1 validation

## 1. Product

**I3DION Spatial**

## 2. Category

Industrial AR Catalog & Sales Visualization Platform.

## 3. Product Vision

Help industrial organizations transform product information into
interactive digital product experiences that improve understanding and
sales engagement.

## 4. Primary Problem

Complex physical products are difficult to communicate spatially through
traditional static sales materials.

## 5. Primary Customers

-   Industrial manufacturers
-   Industrial dealers/distributors
-   Sales organizations
-   Product marketing teams

## 6. Primary Users

-   Sales engineers
-   Product marketing/product owners
-   Administrators
-   Industrial prospects/customers

## 7. Core User Journey

**Create Product → Add Assets → Publish → Create/Share QR → Customer
Opens Experience → Explore Product → 3D/AR → CTA → Sales Inquiry**

## 8. MVP Capability Areas

### Product Management

-   Product creation/editing
-   Specifications
-   Images/media
-   3D asset support

### Catalog

-   Product selection
-   Catalog organization
-   Preview
-   Publish

### QR

-   Product/catalog QR
-   Sharing/download

### Product Experience

-   Product overview
-   Specifications
-   Media
-   Documents

### 3D

-   Rotate
-   Zoom
-   Inspect

### AR

-   Placement
-   Move
-   Rotate
-   Scale
-   Lock/unlock
-   Reset

### Demonstration

-   Animation playback where supported

### Sales

-   Request quote
-   Contact sales
-   Inquiry/lead capture

These capabilities align with the current PRD baseline and feature
specification.

## 9. Product Differentiation Hypothesis

The product may differentiate by combining: **industrial context +
managed product/catalog workflow + interactive 3D + AR + QR access +
sales actions + engagement measurement.**

This must be validated through competitive research.

## 10. Business Model Hypothesis

The product is intended as a B2B product/service. Exact pricing,
packaging, contract model, and buyer economics require market
validation.

## 11. MVP Success

The MVP should prove an end-to-end business loop:

**Company → Product → Catalog/Experience → QR/Link → Customer → 3D/AR →
Sales Action**

## 12. Key Risks

-   Customer willingness to pay
-   Adoption by sales teams
-   3D asset availability/quality
-   AR reliability
-   Browser/device compatibility
-   Enterprise security expectations
-   Content maintenance burden
-   Competitive differentiation

## 13. Phase 1 Questions

1.  Who has the strongest pain?
2.  Who pays?
3.  What is the highest-value use case?
4.  Which products are best suited?
5.  What existing tools compete with the workflow?
6.  What is the minimum lovable MVP?
7.  What evidence is required before launch?
