# I3DION Spatial --- Product Decision Log

**Document ID:** PS-DEC-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Controlled decision register

## 1. Purpose

The decision log records significant product choices so that future
contributors can understand not only what was decided, but why.

## 2. Decision Record Format

Each decision should contain: - Decision ID - Date - Context -
Decision - Alternatives considered - Rationale - Impact - Owner -
Status - Review date if applicable

## 3. Initial Decisions

### PD-001 --- Industrial Product Visualization Is the Core Category

**Status:** Working/approved baseline

I3DION Spatial is positioned as an Industrial AR Catalog & Sales
Visualization Platform.

**Reason:** This is the central theme across the existing PRD and design
baseline.

### PD-002 --- 3D Is a Core MVP Capability

**Status:** Approved baseline

Interactive 3D product visualization is included in the documented MVP.

### PD-003 --- AR Is a Core MVP Capability

**Status:** Approved baseline

AR is included to provide spatial visualization.

### PD-004 --- QR Is a Core Access Mechanism

**Status:** Approved baseline

QR provides a low-friction route into product/catalog experiences.

### PD-005 --- Catalog Management Is Core

**Status:** Approved baseline

The product needs a structured workflow for organizing and publishing
products.

### PD-006 --- Sales Actions Are Core

**Status:** Approved baseline

Request Quote, Contact Sales, and inquiry/lead workflows connect product
engagement to business value.

### PD-007 --- Advanced Capabilities Are Not Automatically MVP

**Status:** Approved principle

Advanced AI, simulation, complex configurators, and other expansion
capabilities require separate validation.

### PD-008 --- Product Decisions Require Traceability

**Status:** Approved

Major scope decisions must be documented and traceable to requirements
and implementation.

## 4. Decision Governance

A decision can be: - Proposed - Under Review - Approved - Rejected -
Superseded

## 5. Change Rule

When a decision changes: 1. Create a new decision record. 2. Reference
the old decision. 3. Explain why it changed. 4. Assess
product/design/engineering/business impact. 5. Update affected
documentation. 6. Update GitHub issues/milestones if applicable.

## 6. Decision Quality Rule

A decision is not considered strong merely because it is technically
convenient. Product decisions should consider customer value, business
value, risk, effort, and strategic fit.
