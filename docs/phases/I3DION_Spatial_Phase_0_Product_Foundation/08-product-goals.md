# I3DION Spatial --- Product Goals

**Document ID:** PS-GOAL-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Goal Framework

Goals should describe outcomes, not merely features.

## G1 --- Improve Industrial Product Understanding

Enable customers to explore products through interactive digital
experiences.

**Outcome indicators:** - Product experience completion - 3D
interaction - AR launch - Customer feedback - Reduced unanswered product
questions

## G2 --- Improve Sales Demonstration

Help sales teams demonstrate products more effectively.

**Outcome indicators:** - Sales-user adoption - Demonstration usage -
Repeat usage - Sales feedback - Lead actions after demonstrations

## G3 --- Digitize Product Catalog Workflows

Allow organizations to create and publish structured digital product
catalogs.

**Outcome indicators:** - Products created - Catalogs published - Time
to publish - Content update frequency

## G4 --- Create a Measurable Engagement Funnel

Connect customer product interaction to measurable business actions.

**Outcome indicators:** - QR scans - Product views - AR launches -
Session duration - Quote/contact actions - Leads

## G5 --- Establish an Enterprise-Ready Foundation

Build reliable authentication, organization boundaries, permissions,
asset handling, observability, and deployment practices appropriate for
the intended B2B product.

## G6 --- Validate Product-Market Fit Before Broad Scale

Do not optimize for large-scale infrastructure before confirming that
customers value the workflow.

## 2. Goal Hierarchy

**Business Outcome → Product Outcome → User Behaviour → Feature**

Example:

**More qualified sales conversations → More product engagement →
Customers explore 3D/AR → 3D/AR viewer**

## 3. Goal Non-Substitution

A completed feature is not evidence of goal achievement.

We measure whether the feature changes user behaviour or business
outcome.
