## Persona 1 --- Sales Engineer

**Role:** Industrial Sales Engineer\
**Primary goal:** Demonstrate products clearly and move qualified
prospects toward a commercial conversation.

### Context

Works with customers in meetings, demonstrations, site visits,
exhibitions, or remote discussions.

### Goals

-   Explain product value quickly
-   Show physical scale
-   Demonstrate important components
-   Answer product questions
-   Generate a sales inquiry

### Pain Points

-   Static material is difficult to use for spatial explanation
-   Physical demonstrations are not always available
-   Repeated explanations consume time
-   Customer engagement is difficult to quantify

### Desired Experience

**Open → Select Product → Explore → 3D/AR → Explain → CTA → Lead**

### Success

Customer understands the product and takes a next action.

## Persona 2 --- Product Marketing Manager

**Role:** Product Marketing / Digital Product Owner

### Goals

-   Maintain accurate product information
-   Manage media and 3D assets
-   Build catalogs
-   Publish product experiences
-   Maintain consistent branding

### Pain Points

-   Product information can be fragmented
-   Updating traditional materials can be slow
-   Sales teams may use outdated assets
-   Digital engagement may be difficult to measure

### Desired Experience

**Create Product → Add Specifications/Assets → Build Catalog → Publish →
Monitor**

### Success

Accurate product experiences are published quickly and consistently.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Persona 3 --- Industrial Customer / Decision Maker

**Role:** Prospect evaluating equipment

### Goals

-   Understand product capabilities
-   Determine suitability
-   Understand physical scale
-   Compare alternatives
-   Contact sales when interested

### Pain Points

-   Product brochures may not communicate physical context
-   Questions may remain after a sales presentation
-   Technical information can be difficult to visualize

### Desired Experience

**Scan/Open → Understand → Explore → Visualize → Evaluate → Request
Quote**

### Success

Higher confidence and a clear next step.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Persona 4 --- Facility Manager

**Role:** Facility / Operations Manager

### Goals

-   Evaluate equipment placement
-   Understand space implications
-   Review product information
-   Discuss installation feasibility

### Pain Points

-   Static dimensions require mental visualization
-   Equipment placement is difficult to communicate remotely

### Desired Experience

**Select Product → View Dimensions/3D → AR → Evaluate Space → Discuss**

### Success

Reduced uncertainty around physical context.

## Persona Validation Plan

For each persona, interview at least several real users before calling
the persona validated.

Validate: - Job frequency - Current workflow - Pain severity - Existing
alternatives - Decision authority - Willingness to adopt - Willingness
to pay / economic influence
