# I3DION Spatial --- Product Principles

**Document ID:** PS-PRN-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Working principles

## P1 --- Problem Before Feature

A feature must trace to a user problem or validated business
requirement.

## P2 --- Customer Outcome Over Technology

3D, AR, AI, WebXR, or any other technology is an implementation means,
not the product outcome.

## P3 --- Visualization Is Core

The product should remain strongly associated with industrial product
understanding and demonstration.

## P4 --- Simple for the User, Powerful for the Organization

Customer-facing interactions should be simple. Administrative workflows
can expose necessary enterprise controls without overwhelming the end
user.

## P5 --- Fast Path to Value

The path from QR/link to product experience should have minimal
unnecessary friction.

## P6 --- Sales Enablement

The product should help a user move from understanding to a commercial
next action.

## P7 --- Measurable by Design

Important journeys should generate reliable product telemetry while
respecting privacy and security requirements.

## P8 --- Enterprise Trust

The experience should communicate professionalism, reliability,
security, consistency, and enterprise readiness.

## P9 --- Accessibility and Usability

Core interactions should be understandable, keyboard-accessible where
applicable, responsive, and usable across supported devices.

The current design system specifies a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1,
keyboard navigation support, and responsive design.

## P10 --- Scalable but Not Over-Engineered

Build a foundation that supports growth, but do not prematurely optimize
for hypothetical scale.

## P11 --- Controlled Scope

Every roadmap item competes for limited time and resources.

## P12 --- Evidence-Driven Decisions

Customer evidence, product analytics, business constraints, and
engineering reality should inform prioritization.

## P13 --- Traceability

Every major requirement should be traceable:

**Problem → Requirement → Design → Issue → PR → Test → Release →
Metric**

## P14 --- Consistency

The product should maintain a coherent interaction model across product
management, catalogs, QR experiences, 3D, AR, and sales workflows.

## P15 --- Security by Design

Identity, authorization, tenant boundaries, asset access, secrets,
logging, and auditability should be considered from the beginning rather
than added after launch.
