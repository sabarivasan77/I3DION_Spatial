# I3DION Spatial --- Target Customer Definition

**Document ID:** PS-CUS-001\
**Version:** 0.2\
**Status:** Draft / Research Required

## 1. Primary Customer Segments

### A. Industrial Manufacturers

Companies producing physical industrial equipment such as: -
Compressors - Pumps - Generators - Machinery - Automation equipment -
Process equipment - Engineering products

**Likely need:** Digital product presentation, centralized product
information, interactive demonstration, and sales enablement.

### B. Industrial Dealers & Distributors

Organizations selling industrial products from one or more
manufacturers.

**Likely need:** A fast way to present products to prospects without
carrying physical demonstration assets.

### C. Sales Organizations / Sales Engineers

Customer-facing teams that demonstrate products.

**Likely need:** A reliable presentation workflow with product
visualization and sales CTAs.

### D. Product Marketing Teams

Teams responsible for product communication and digital sales assets.

**Likely need:** Product asset management, catalogs, publishing, and
consistent customer-facing experiences.

## 2. Secondary User Groups

The current product baseline also identifies: - Architects -
Consultants - Facility managers - End customers / decision makers

These groups may consume product experiences without being the primary
buyer of the platform.

## 3. Buyer vs User

A key PM distinction:

  Role                      Relationship
  ------------------------- -------------------------------
  Economic Buyer            Approves budget
  Product/Marketing Owner   Maintains product content
  Sales Engineer            Uses experience in selling
  Administrator             Manages organization/platform
  Customer/Prospect         Consumes product experience
  Technical Stakeholder     Evaluates technical fit

The exact roles must be validated with customer research.

## 4. Ideal Customer Profile --- Initial Hypothesis

A high-fit customer may have: - Complex physical products - A B2B sales
process - Multiple sales engineers/dealers - Existing digital product
assets - A need for demonstrations - A measurable sales funnel -
Willingness to invest in digital sales enablement

## 5. Customer Qualification Questions

A prospect should be investigated for: 1. Number of products 2. Product
complexity 3. Sales cycle length 4. Demonstration frequency 5. Current
catalog tools 6. Current 3D/AR usage 7. Digital asset maturity 8. Number
of sales users 9. CRM/ERP environment 10. Security/compliance
requirements 11. Budget owner 12. Adoption barriers

## 6. Segmentation

Segment customers by: - Industry - Product complexity - Company size -
Sales model - Number of SKUs - Geographic sales coverage - Digital
maturity - Demonstration intensity

## 7. Priority Rule

Do not assume the largest company is the best customer. Prioritize
segments where the problem is: **Frequent + Painful + Valuable +
Solvable + Budgeted.**

## 8. Validation Status

Current segments are based on existing product documentation and product
hypotheses. ICP ranking, buyer roles, willingness-to-pay, and purchasing
process require discovery.
