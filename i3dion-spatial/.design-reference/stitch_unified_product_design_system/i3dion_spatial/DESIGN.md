I3DION Spatial
Design System Document (DSD)
Version 1.0
1. Design Philosophy
Design Goal
I3DION Spatial is an Industrial AR Catalog & Sales Visualization Platform.
The interface should communicate:
Trust
Professionalism
Innovation
Simplicity
Enterprise Readiness
The design language should feel similar to:
Notion (Clean)
Linear (Modern)
Stripe Dashboard (Professional)
Autodesk Products (Industrial)
2. Brand Identity
Product Name
I3DION Spatial
Brand Personality
Professional
Modern
Industrial
Technical
Clean

8. Button System
Primary Button: Spatial Blue (#2563EB), White text, 12px radius, 48px height.
Secondary Button: White bg, Gray border, Dark Gray text.
Danger Button: Red bg, White text.
9. Input Fields
Standard Input: 48px height, 12px radius, 1px Gray border.
10. Final Design Principle
Every component must prioritize clarity, speed, and product visualization. Maintain consistency across catalog creation, product management, QR experiences, AR visualization, and sales workflows.
